## 🚀 Getting Started

### 1. Accessing the Dashboard

- Navigate to the **landing page** of the Grand Slam Offer Generator
- Click on action buttons like **"Watch AI Build Your Offer Live"** or **"Get Started Free"**
- If you're not signed in, you'll be prompted to authenticate via Supabase
- Upon successful sign-in, you'll be redirected to the main **Dashboard**

### 2. Authentication Options

- **Sign Up**: Create a new account with email and password
- **Sign In**: Use existing credentials
- **Social Login**: Connect via supported OAuth providers (if configured)

## 📝 Creating Your Offer

### Step 1: Input Business Details

Fill out the stylish input form with the following required fields:

- **Business Type** 
  - *Example*: "Online Fitness Coaching", "SaaS Productivity Tool", "E-commerce Store"
- **Target Market** 
  - *Example*: "Busy professionals aged 30-45", "Small business owners", "College students"
- **Main Problem** 
  - *Example*: "No time for gym, complicated diet plans", "Inefficient team communication"
- **Revenue Goal** 
  - *Example*: "$10,000/month", "$100K annually", "$1M in 12 months"

💡 **Pro Tip**: Be specific and detailed in your inputs for better AI-generated results.

### Step 2: Generate Your Grand Slam Offer

- Click the **"Generate My Grand Slam Offer"** button
- Enjoy the engaging **animated presentation** that guides you through the AI generation process
- Visual progress indicators keep you entertained while the AI crafts your custom offer
- The generation process typically takes 30-60 seconds

## 🔍 Exploring Your Generated Offer

### Offer Structure

Your generated offer is divided into **11 core components** based on Hormozi's framework:

1. **Core Offer** - The main value proposition
2. **Bonuses** - Additional value stacks
3. **Scarcity** - Urgency and limitation tactics
4. **Urgency** - Time-sensitive elements
5. **Guarantees** - Risk reversal strategies
6. **Pricing Strategy** - Optimal pricing structure
7. **Payment Terms** - Flexible payment options
8. **Delivery Method** - How value is delivered
9. **Naming Convention** - Compelling offer names
10. **Social Proof** - Credibility builders
11. **Call to Action** - Conversion elements

### Free vs. Pro User Experience

#### 🆓 Free Users
- **Preview Access**: See the first 3 items of each component
- **Blurred Content**: Additional items appear blurred with motivational messaging:
  > "There are 20+ items, buy the offer to unlock all and export it."
- **Upgrade Prompts**: Strategic buy buttons on each component and at the bottom
- **Limited Views**: Access to basic list view only

#### 💎 Pro Users
- **Full Access**: All generated items for each component are visible
- **Professional PDF Export**: Download beautifully formatted offers
- **Premium Views**: Access to all three viewing modes
- **Premium Badges**: Visual indicators of Pro status
- **No Restrictions**: Complete offer exploration without limitations

## 📤 Exporting Your Offer (Pro Feature)

### PDF Export Process

1. Navigate to any view (List, Text, or Mindmap)
2. Click the **"Export PDF"** button
3. Choose your export options:
   - **Full Offer**: Complete offer with all components
   - **Selected Components**: Choose specific sections
   - **Branding Options**: Include/exclude logos and styling
4. Download your professionally formatted PDF

### Export Features

- **Professional Formatting**: Clean, business-ready layout
