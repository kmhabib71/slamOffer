## Why This Works: Delivery Vehicle Psychology

### 1. Multiple Entry Points

Different delivery vehicles serve different customer segments, budgets, and preferences, maximizing market coverage.

### 2. Upgrade Path Creation

Natural progression from free/low-cost options to premium services creates customer lifetime value optimization.

### 3. Risk Reduction

Variety of options reduces risk for customers who can start small and scale up as they see results.

### 4. Authority Building

Multiple delivery methods demonstrate expertise and establish market leadership position.
