## 📞 Sales Process

### Sales Methodology: MEDDIC + Value Selling

#### M - Metrics

- What metrics matter most to the prospect?
- How do they currently measure lead generation success?
- What's their target for lead volume/quality?

#### E - Economic Buyer

- Who controls the budget for marketing/sales tools?
- What's their decision-making process?
- What's their budget range?

#### D - Decision Criteria

- What criteria will they use to evaluate solutions?
- What are their must-haves vs. nice-to-haves?
- How do they measure ROI?

#### D - Decision Process

- Who else is involved in the decision?
- What's their timeline for implementation?
- What could delay or accelerate the decision?

#### I - Identify Pain

- What's their biggest lead generation challenge?
- How much is this problem costing them?
- What happens if they don't solve it?

#### C - Champion

- Who internally will advocate for our solution?
- Do they have influence with the economic buyer?
- How can we enable them to sell internally?

### Sales Process Stages

#### Stage 1: Initial Contact (Day 1)

**Objective:** Qualify opportunity and book discovery call
**Activities:**

- Send personalized connection message
- Share relevant case study
- Book 15-minute discovery call

**Qualifying Questions:**

- "How do you currently generate leads?"
- "What's working well and what isn't?"
- "What would a 50% increase in qualified leads mean for your business?"

#### Stage 2: Discovery Call (Day 3-7)

**Objective:** Understand pain points and qualify budget/timeline
**Duration:** 30 minutes
**Agenda:**

1. Current lead generation challenges (10 min)
2. Impact of solving these challenges (10 min)
3. Solution overview (5 min)
4. Next steps (5 min)

**Key Questions:**

- "Walk me through your current lead generation process"
- "What's your cost per lead across different channels?"
- "How do you track ROI on your marketing investments?"
- "What would need to happen for this to be successful?"

#### Stage 3: Demo Call (Day 10-14)

**Objective:** Show specific value for their use case
**Duration:** 45 minutes
**Structure:**

1. Recap discovery insights (5 min)
2. Customized demo (30 min)
3. ROI calculation (5 min)
4. Next steps discussion (5 min)

**Demo Flow:**

- Show their current challenges in our system
- Demonstrate how we solve each pain point
- Calculate potential ROI based on their numbers
- Address any concerns or objections

#### Stage 4: Proposal & Negotiation (Day 17-21)

**Objective:** Present tailored proposal and handle objections
**Deliverables:**

- Custom proposal document
- ROI analysis spreadsheet
- Implementation timeline
- References from similar customers

**Common Objections & Responses:**

- "Too expensive" → Show ROI calculation and cost of inaction
- "Need to think about it" → Identify specific concerns and address
- "Happy with current solution" → Highlight unique differentiation
- "Need approval" → Offer to present to decision makers

#### Stage 5: Closing (Day 24-30)

**Objective:** Finalize agreement and start onboarding
**Activities:**

- Final negotiation on terms
- Contract execution
- Onboarding kickoff scheduling
- Success criteria definition

**Closing Techniques:**

- **Assumptive Close**: "When would you like to start onboarding?"
- **Alternative Close**: "Would you prefer monthly or annual billing?"
- **Urgency Close**: "We have implementation slots available next week"

### Sales Enablement Tools

**Inputs:**

**Outputs:**
