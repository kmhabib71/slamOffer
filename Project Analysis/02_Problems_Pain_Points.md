## Core Problem Categories (Hormozi's 4 Value Drivers)

### 1. 🎯 DREAM OUTCOME Problems

**Core Issue**: Entrepreneurs can't clearly define or articulate compelling dream outcomes

**Specific Problems:**

- Creating vague or generic desired outcomes
- Not making the dream outcome specific and measurable
- Focusing on features instead of meaningful outcomes
- Unable to connect their service to the customer's ultimate goal
- Creating unrealistic or unachievable dream outcomes
- Don't understand what "Dream Outcome" means in practice
- Offer is feature-focused, not transformation-focused
- Can't describe the before/after transformation for customers

### 2. ⏰ TIME DELAY Problems

**Core Issue**: Customers perceive it will take too long to see results

**Specific Problems:**

- Not knowing how to provide quick wins early in the process
- Creating long implementation timelines that discourage buyers
- Unable to break down results into achievable milestones
- Not providing immediate value or gratification
- Struggling to balance thoroughness with speed
- Offer doesn't promise quick wins or fast results
- Transformation seems far away with no immediate benefits
- No fast gratification elements in the offer structure

### 3. 💪 EFFORT & SACRIFICE Problems

**Core Issue**: Offers require too much work from customers

**Specific Problems:**

- Not knowing how to make their solution easier to consume
- Requiring too much work and effort from customers
- Creating complex implementation processes
- Not providing adequate support or hand-holding
- Failing to remove friction from the customer experience
- High learning curve that overwhelms prospects
- Customers feel overwhelmed before even buying
- Poor balance between education and execution

### 4. 🎲 PERCEIVED LIKELIHOOD Problems

**Core Issue**: Prospects don't believe the offer will work for them

**Specific Problems:**

- Lack of credible proof elements (testimonials, case studies, credentials)
- Not knowing how to build trust with skeptical prospects
- Unable to demonstrate track record effectively
- Struggling to overcome industry skepticism or past bad experiences
- Not addressing the "too good to be true" objection preemptively
- No testimonials, reviews, or case studies to show
- No authority or credibility demonstrated
- Audience doesn't trust them to deliver results
