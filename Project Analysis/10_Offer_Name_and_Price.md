## PSYCHOLOGICAL PRICING PRINCIPLES

### 1. Anchoring Strategy

Present highest-value option first to set price expectations:

- **Enterprise**: $2,997/month (anchor)
- **Professional**: $997/month (looks reasonable)
- **Starter**: $497/month (appears like a steal)

### 2. Decoy Effect

Make middle option most attractive:

- **Starter**: Good value
- **Professional**: BEST VALUE (most popular)
- **Enterprise**: Premium option

### 3. Odd-Even Pricing Psychology

- **$497**: Feels like a deal (under $500)
- **$997**: Serious but attainable (under $1,000)
- **$2,997**: Premium but not ridiculous (under $3,000)

### 4. Value Stacking

Always show total value vs. price:

- **Starter**: $15,000 value for $497
- **Professional**: $35,000 value for $997
- **Enterprise**: $75,000 value for $2,997

### 5. Payment Psychology

Frame monthly payments as daily cost:

- **Starter**: "Less than $17/day"
- **Professional**: "Less than $33/day"
- **Enterprise**: "Less than $100/day"

## PRICING COMMUNICATION STRATEGY

### Price Reveal Sequence

#### Step 1: Build Value First

Present all components and benefits before mentioning price

#### Step 2: Anchor with Alternatives

"Hiring a consultant would cost $50,000+"
"Learning this yourself would take 6 months"
"Most AI tools charge $500+ monthly"

#### Step 3: Stack Total Value

"If you bought all these components separately, you'd pay $35,000+"

#### Step 4: Reveal Price with Contrast

"But you won't pay $35,000, or even $10,000..."
"Your investment is just $997/month"

#### Step 5: Add Bonus Incentives

"Plus, if you join today, you get..."

#### Step 6: Include Guarantee

"And it's completely risk-free with our 60-day guarantee"

### Price Objection Handling

#### "It's too expensive":

**Response**: "I understand - let me ask you this: What's it costing you to NOT have irresistible offers? Most entrepreneurs leave $100,000+ on the table annually with weak offers. This pays for itself in the first month."

#### "I can't afford it":

**Response**: "I hear you - that's exactly why you need this. We have payment plans available, and remember, you get your money back if it doesn't work. The question is: Can you afford to keep struggling with offers that don't convert?"

#### "I need to think about it":

**Response**: "Absolutely - this is an important decision. What specific concerns do you have? I'd rather address them now than have you wonder later."

#### "I want to compare other options":

**Response**: "Smart approach - I encourage you to compare. You'll find that we offer more value, better support, and stronger guarantees than anyone else. Plus, we have a 60-day guarantee, so you can compare risk-free."

## NAMING AND PRICING TESTING

### A/B Testing Framework

#### Name Testing Variables:

- Emotional vs. logical appeals
- Benefit vs. feature focus
- Length (short vs. descriptive)
- Industry terms vs. plain language

#### Price Testing Variables:

- Price points ($497 vs. $597 vs. $697)
- Payment terms (monthly vs. annual)
- Bundling options
- Discount structures

#### Testing Metrics:

- Click-through rates on headlines
- Conversion rates at different price points
- Customer lifetime value by segment
- Refund rates and satisfaction scores

### Optimization Timeline

#### Week 1-2: Baseline Testing

Test current name and pricing against one alternative

#### Week 3-4: Price Point Testing

Test 3 different price points for each tier

#### Week 5-6: Bundle Testing

Test different component combinations

#### Week 7-8: Messaging Testing

Test different value propositions and positioning

#### Week 9-10: Integration Testing

Test best-performing elements together
