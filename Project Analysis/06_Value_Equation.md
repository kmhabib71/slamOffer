## Why This Works: Value Equation Psychology

### 1. Rational Decision Making

The value equation appeals to logical thinking by clearly showing the mathematical benefit of choosing your solution.

### 2. Risk vs. Reward Analysis

High perceived likelihood reduces fear and builds confidence in the purchase decision.
